# 07 — Website and patient experience

## 1. Public website objectives

- Establish a trustworthy, accurate online presence.
- Explain verified hospital, practitioner and pharmacy information.
- Make phone, directions and appointment enquiry easy.
- Present treatments in clear language without unsupported efficacy claims.
- Support English and Malayalam content if the client can maintain both.
- Improve local search visibility through accurate business information.

## 2. Proposed pages

1. Home.
2. About the establishment.
3. Practitioner profile(s), qualifications and registration details as verified.
4. Consultation information.
5. Ayurveda treatments/therapies actually offered.
6. Pharmacy information and product categories.
7. Appointment enquiry.
8. Patient visit information and FAQs.
9. Contact, map and opening hours.
10. Privacy notice, terms and accessibility information.

## 3. Appointment enquiry

A website request should collect only the minimum necessary contact and scheduling information. It must be labelled as a request, not a confirmed appointment, until staff accept it. Avoid collecting detailed medical histories through a public form. Provide a secure channel for clinical information if needed.

## 4. Patient portal/app — validate before build

Potential features:
- View and request appointments.
- Receive appointment and therapy reminders.
- View documents or prescriptions made available by the establishment.
- View follow-up instructions.
- Manage communication preferences.
- Contact the establishment.

The portal should not expose practitioner-only notes or allow patients to modify clinical records. Proxy/family access, minors and identity verification require explicit policy decisions.

## 5. Local presence

Verify and standardize:
- Google Business Profile name, address, map pin and hours.
- Public phone numbers and website domain.
- Photos with appropriate permissions.
- Service descriptions and categories.
- Review response process that does not disclose patient information.
- Local search phrases for Muttom and Thodupuzha.

## 6. Content governance

The client must approve all names, qualifications, service descriptions, timings, fees, photographs, claims and contact details. Clinical content must be reviewed by the responsible practitioner. Never publish patient testimonials or images without documented permission.
