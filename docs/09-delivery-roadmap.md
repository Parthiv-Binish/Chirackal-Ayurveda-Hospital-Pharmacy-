# 09 — Delivery roadmap

**Planning only:** Dates, costs and staffing must be estimated after site discovery.

## Phase 0 — Discovery and validation
- Interview management, practitioner, reception, therapy staff and pharmacy staff.
- Inspect blank forms/registers and current workflow.
- Record volumes, hardware, connectivity and constraints.
- Verify registration/licensing questions with client.
- Agree success metrics, budget and project owner.

**Exit:** Signed discovery report and prioritized requirements.

## Phase 1 — Foundation
- Public website and verified business information.
- Authentication, users, groups and permissions.
- Patient registry and reception workflow.
- Appointment and walk-in queue.
- Practitioner consultation and prescription records.
- Basic hospital billing and receipts.

**Exit:** Staff can complete a representative outpatient journey with auditability.

## Phase 2 — Pharmacy
- Catalogue, suppliers and purchase receiving.
- Batch/expiry-aware stock ledger.
- Prescription-linked dispensing.
- Independent retail sales.
- Returns, adjustments, stock count and alerts.
- Pharmacy billing and daily reconciliation.

**Exit:** Sample stock and sales reconciliation passes with pharmacy lead.

## Phase 3 — Therapy operations
- Practitioner therapy orders.
- Resource/staff scheduling.
- Session attendance and completion.
- Follow-up and progress review.

**Exit:** End-to-end therapy plan and session workflow approved by clinical lead.

## Phase 4 — Management and communications
- Operational and financial dashboards.
- Daily close and exports.
- Appointment/follow-up reminders with communication preferences.
- WhatsApp/SMS integration only after provider, cost and consent review.

**Exit:** Management validates report figures against source records.

## Phase 5 — Patient-facing digital services
- Patient portal/app only after identity, privacy and adoption assessment.
- Appointment requests and status.
- Approved documents and reminders.
- Patient communication preferences.

**Exit:** Security, usability and patient-support testing complete.

## Phase 6 — Optimization and integrations
- ABDM feasibility and optional integration.
- Additional locations only if confirmed.
- Advanced analytics and workflow automation.
- Online pharmacy fulfilment only after legal and operational review.

## Delivery controls

- Maintain a prioritized issue/backlog list.
- Use separate development, staging and production environments.
- Require review and automated tests for critical workflows.
- Use synthetic data in non-production environments.
- Record decisions and client approvals.
- Train staff by role and provide quick-reference guides.
- Track adoption and support issues after launch.
