# 10 — Site discovery checklist

Use this checklist during an on-site session. Mark each item as confirmed, not applicable or follow-up. Do not record patient names, phone numbers, medical records or other identifiable health information in this repository.

## A. Business and legal structure
- [ ] Legal entity/business name and ownership contact.
- [ ] Hospital and pharmacy relationship: same entity or separate.
- [ ] Registration and licence types, numbers and validity verified privately.
- [ ] Address, map pin, public phone and opening hours.
- [ ] Branches/counters and operating days.
- [ ] Responsible management contact.

## B. Clinical service model
- [ ] Practitioner count, qualifications and registration verified.
- [ ] Consultation hours, appointment slots and walk-in policy.
- [ ] Daily/weekly patient volumes.
- [ ] Consultation types and fees.
- [ ] Consultation documentation and prescription template.
- [ ] Treatments/therapies actually offered.
- [ ] Treatment room/resource count.
- [ ] Therapist/staff responsibilities.
- [ ] Therapy duration, scheduling and cancellation practices.
- [ ] Day-care or observation facilities, if any (inpatient admissions are not provided).
- [ ] Follow-up process and clinical record retention policy.

## C. Reception and patient records
- [ ] New patient registration steps.
- [ ] Returning patient search method.
- [ ] Patient identifier currently used, if any.
- [ ] Appointment book and queue handling.
- [ ] Patient contact and communication preferences.
- [ ] Blank/de-identified forms collected.
- [ ] Duplicate and correction process.

## D. Pharmacy — prescription-linked and retail
- [ ] Confirm both dispensing and independent retail sales.
- [ ] Same or separate counter, premises, legal entity and stock.
- [ ] Responsible pharmacist/person and applicable permissions.
- [ ] Product categories and approximate catalogue size.
- [ ] Units, pack sizes and product coding/barcodes.
- [ ] Supplier list and purchase workflow.
- [ ] Batch/lot and expiry tracking.
- [ ] Storage requirements and controlled access.
- [ ] Prescription verification and dispensing steps.
- [ ] Retail sale steps without a hospital encounter.
- [ ] Partial supply, substitutions and out-of-stock process.
- [ ] Returns, damaged/expired stock and adjustments.
- [ ] Purchase invoices, sales bills and stock registers.
- [ ] Daily sales and stock reconciliation.

## E. Finance
- [ ] Hospital and pharmacy bill numbering.
- [ ] Consultation/treatment/service charges.
- [ ] Product pricing and discount approval.
- [ ] Cash, UPI, card and bank payment methods.
- [ ] Split/partial payments and refunds.
- [ ] Daily cash close and variance handling.
- [ ] Expenses, supplier payments and accounting software/process.
- [ ] Accountant review of tax/invoice requirements.

## F. People and permissions
- [ ] Staff list and job functions (store securely outside this public repo if sensitive).
- [ ] Who can register patients?
- [ ] Who can view/edit clinical notes?
- [ ] Who can issue/finalize prescriptions?
- [ ] Who can schedule/record therapies?
- [ ] Who can dispense medicines?
- [ ] Who can edit stock/prices?
- [ ] Who can apply discounts/refunds?
- [ ] Who can view/export financial reports?
- [ ] Owner approval and access review process.

## G. Infrastructure
- [ ] Computer/tablet count and location.
- [ ] Printer, barcode scanner and receipt printer.
- [ ] Internet provider, reliability and backup connectivity.
- [ ] Power backup.
- [ ] Existing network/Wi-Fi and access restrictions.
- [ ] Existing domain, email and social accounts.
- [ ] Backup and current data storage practice.
- [ ] Staff device constraints and digital literacy.

## H. Project governance
- [ ] Named client decision-maker.
- [ ] Clinical requirements approver.
- [ ] Pharmacy requirements approver.
- [ ] Finance requirements approver.
- [ ] Budget range and procurement constraints.
- [ ] Target rollout expectations.
- [ ] Support and maintenance owner.
- [ ] Success metrics and baseline measurements.

## Site visit outputs

1. Verified business profile.
2. AS-IS workflow diagrams.
3. Register/form inventory.
4. Roles and responsibility matrix.
5. De-identified sample data and field dictionary.
6. Infrastructure assessment.
7. Compliance questions and responsible reviewer.
8. Prioritized requirements and exclusions.
9. Risks, dependencies and open decisions.
10. Client-approved next-step plan.
